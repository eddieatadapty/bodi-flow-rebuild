Day 1

Pass the Meta campaign into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the campaign each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per promise

INSANITY Unhinged, the 30-day challenge and the GLP-1 plan each get their own first screens; installs from anywhere else keep today’s welcome.

Week 2

Judge each ad on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that ad earns, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three of these ads link to the web today: “100 Days” and “30 Days” to bodi.com, the GLP-1 video to shakeology.com. Most of your other Meta ads sell Shakeology, P90X and Belle Vitale supplements, so they have no phone here. We found no Apple Ads running for BODi in the US over the last 30 days, so your own name is open to other apps. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
